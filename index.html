    Export

    Exports try 20 fps first, then 12 fps. If it's still over 5 MB, the footage is compressed in steps — Light, Medium, Strong, Heavy, Maximum — stopping at the lightest step that fits. Compression is lossy (fewer colors, and pixels that barely changed are reused from the previous frame), but size, duration and frame rate never change. Every result is checked against the spec before download is offered.
